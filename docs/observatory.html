Lop Nur Twin · ECHO & R.A.I.N.

A simulated world with a persistent digital fly, sealed histories and a separate laboratory that tests whether changes to research subjects improve behavior.

Observe ECHO, inspect continuity records, and challenge the intelligence experiments.

EXECUTABLE · OBSERVED

Evidence, limits & next experiment

What happens when agents have to live somewhere?

  • EXECUTABLE — public-source reconstruction, labelled-fiction games, integrated R.A.I.N. research runtime and bounded civilization experiments; persistent ECHO runtime with sealed checkpoint continuation and a separate intelligence metrology bench
  • OBSERVED — scripted Bethesda and rule-based civilization experiments with native replay; one live Gemma-designed oscillator run remained inconclusive after numerical guards failed; no physical or general scientific validation; recorded ECHO continuity interval checks and held-out simulated plasticity experiments with inconclusive benefit

Still open

Reconstruction cannot confirm the real site. Simulator findings do not establish human behavior, historical outcomes, physical measurements or general scientific intelligence. The retained live Gemma oscillator experiment failed its analytic accuracy guards. The original ECHO and Eva are not controlled by the FlyWire/BANC research backends. Continuity checks cover a stated interval, not full lifetime proof or consciousness. Active embodied plasticity has no established performance advantage in the published held-out study.

Next experiment

A new preregistered numerical investigation meeting its independent accuracy guards, and repeated model-designed studies compared against stated baselines. For the persistent-fly work: an independently audited continuation and a new preregistered held-out comparison that can distinguish benefit, regression and inconclusive evidence without experimenting on either original.

    Quantum Noise Profiler

    A Python instrument that tracks qubit noise with separated provenance and sequential drift tests, and withholds estimates its data cannot support.

    Step through a real IBM qubit pilot, batch by batch.

    EXECUTABLE · OBSERVED · SUPPORTED · NOT SUPPORTED

    Evidence, limits & next experiment

    What would have to be measured?

    • EXECUTABLE — qubit-noise monitoring, provenance-separated series, model/forecast refusal, and declared session error budgets
    • OBSERVED — preregistered v0.2/v0.3 simulations with frozen protocols and stored raw data; one preregistered two-hour assumption-check pilot on IBM ibm_fez (5 qubits, 14 jobs, 296,000 shots), not a detection test
    • SUPPORTED — preregistered pilot check P.C2 on physical hardware: readout stayed stable within single sweep jobs on one IBM device over two hours (0 of 10 sweeps disagreed; at most 1 allowed); an assumption check, not detection performance
    • NOT SUPPORTED — preregistered pilot check P.C1: readout-probe counts did not follow one constant binomial rate across 12 batches (6 of 10 series rejected; at most 1 allowed)

    Still open

    Hardware warning accuracy is not established: the pilot covers one device for two hours, and whether its alerts were true or false is unknown. Guarantees depend on channel-specific null assumptions, and calibrated empirical configurations are distinct from anytime-valid thresholds. Conventional CUSUM matched v0.3 operational performance in simulation.

    Next experiment

    Independent replication and an authorized, separately scoped hardware validation of the monitoring assumptions.
