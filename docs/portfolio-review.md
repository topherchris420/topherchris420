## What makes a project worth leading with

The editorial questions were: does it pose a distinctive, concrete question; is there an implemented experience or instrument someone can inspect; is there a retained record that can contradict its ambitions; and does it reveal a different part of Christopher's practice?

This is an assessment of the work's interest and distinctiveness, **not a systematic prior-art review or a claim of scientific novelty**. Multi-agent research, selective risk, e-processes, replay, retrieval, sensor fusion, and hydrological counterfactuals all have established precedents. The profile emphasizes the questions, combinations, implementation boundaries, and evidence particular to these projects. It does not call established components inventions.

The lead sequence moves from worlds you can enter, to measurements you can challenge, to decisions whose authority you can inspect. R.A.I.N., Pine Gap, Quantum Noise Profiler, and Mannahatta earn early placement for different reasons. James and Pordenone explain the authorization boundary. TRIAD, CIRCLE, and DRR expose the difficult research underneath.
