## Laboratory-wide mandate

**Vers3Dynamics is an experiment in the future organization of scientific discovery.** Its research question is organizational as well as technical: can one human direct a persistent, AI-native laboratory that designs meaningful experiments, confronts negative evidence, and produces knowledge that independent investigators can verify?

The lab is an independent, open scientific effort rather than a venture-backed SaaS business. Research projects are evaluated for experimental value and public reproducibility, not their potential as sales funnels or valuation stories. This is a hypothesis about how research can be organized, not an established claim of superiority.

**Build an AI-native scientific laboratory that reduces confidently wrong decisions and earns the right to make stronger claims through prospective experiments and independent verification.** Protect the difference between working software, measured results, supported conclusions, and externally reproduced discoveries.

The three divisions are research responsibilities rather than GitHub organizations or separate companies. Each public project has one primary placement. Cross-division collaboration is expected.

| Portfolio label | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| **Flagship** | A leading experimental program or evaluation instrument, prioritized for rigorous results |
| **Research** | Active candidate for defined scientific experiments and comparisons |
| **Enabler** | Infrastructure or tool supporting a research program; justify its cost and maintenance |
| **Incubation** | Exploratory work pending a decisive scoped experiment; not a promise of continued investment |
| **Exhibition** | Creative, explanatory, or public-facing work; not evidence of scientific validation |

These are **proposed management priorities**, not the maturity labels in the atlas and not a judgment that any project is already independently validated.

## Shared research infrastructure and boundaries

- **R.A.I.N.**: the scientific-design and evaluation program is Division I; the running software is currently housed in [Lop Nur Twin](https://github.com/topherchris420/lop-nur-twin), which belongs primarily to Division II. The archived [`james_library`](https://github.com/topherchris420/james_library) is not the current runtime.
- **TRIAD, RAIN ATLAS and rain-pipeline**: a common methodological layer for preregistration, abstention, evaluation, and replay. Integration should be proved, not merely announced.
- **Anna, Jasmine, James and embedded validation**: reusable support tools. Each must have a named customer experiment and a comparison against simpler alternatives before expanding its scope.
- **Consent and original-state preservation**: Division III cannot present synthetic physiological testing as human validation; Division II cannot replace or reset Eva/ECHO in order to improve a demonstration.
- **Private repositories**: not included in this public index. Website-only projects are linked to their public experiences without exposing private source.

## Research governance and decision rights

Vers3Dynamics uses a discipline-based research review framework spanning scientific strategy, experimental methodology, interpretability, model and compute efficiency, operational reliability, responsible deployment, and public benefit. These are **review responsibilities**, not claims that particular individuals hold positions in the laboratory.

The laboratory's accountable decision-makers may fund, consolidate, freeze, transfer between divisions, or sunset research lines, including favored projects, only through documented reviews of evidence, dependencies, preservation, and safety. Any delegation of actual authority requires explicit appointments and access controls; until then, repository ownership and operational approval remain with the founder.

Research direction is guided by the strength of the evidence, not by attachment to a project or the number of features delivered.

## Portfolio review rules

1. **Preregister a falsifiable question.** Name the target, success criteria, strong baselines, uncertainty, costs, and data boundaries *before* seeing the confirmatory result.
2. **Publish the whole outcome.** Archive the protocol, code version, seeds, data provenance, failed runs, negative results, and limitations. Never use a successful demonstration as a substitute for a held-out comparison.
3. **Require scientific lift.** Extend a project only where its proposed value survives an appropriate comparison, or where a concrete infrastructure need justifies its existence.
4. **Protect live state.** Preserve experiment records, deployed service continuity, and the separate identities and histories of original digital organisms during maintenance or handoffs.
5. **Sunset explicitly.** To archive a project, first record the reason, last verified state, dependency impact, reproduction instructions, and preservation location. Deprecate interfaces before removing them. A portfolio label alone does not archive anything.
6. **Retain openness with boundaries.** Keep public research inspectable where feasible, but restrict sensitive operations and human-linked data using consent and safety review.
