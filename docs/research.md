### Quantum Noise Profiler

[Project](https://github.com/topherchris420/quantum-noise-profiler) · Reviewed 2026-10-21

- **`EXECUTABLE`** — qubit-noise monitoring, provenance-separated series, model/forecast refusal, and declared session error budgets
- **`OBSERVED`** — preregistered v0.2/v0.3 simulations with frozen protocols and stored raw data; one preregistered two-hour assumption-check pilot on IBM ibm_fez (5 qubits, 14 jobs, 296,000 shots), not a detection test
- **`SUPPORTED`** — preregistered pilot check P.C2 on physical hardware: readout stayed stable within single sweep jobs on one IBM device over two hours (0 of 10 sweeps disagreed; at most 1 allowed); an assumption check, not detection performance
- **`NOT SUPPORTED`** — preregistered pilot check P.C1: readout-probe counts did not follow one constant binomial rate across 12 batches (6 of 10 series rejected; at most 1 allowed)

**Still open:** Hardware warning accuracy is not established: the pilot covers one device for two hours, and whether its alerts were true or false is unknown. Guarantees depend on channel-specific null assumptions, and calibrated empirical configurations are distinct from anytime-valid thresholds. **Next experiment:** Independent replication and an authorized, separately scoped hardware validation of the monitoring assumptions.
