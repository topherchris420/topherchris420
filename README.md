## Start somewhere

### Pine Gap · Give an agent the keys

A night shift, a coffee delivery, a numbers station, and a midnight concert. Explore on foot, drive across the site, or hand the controls to an AI agent. Human and agent actions pass through the same world mechanics; the simulation decides the outcome.

One complete live Jev run is recorded. Paired experiments with scripted agents investigate what makes the task succeed or fail.

[**Play in your browser**](https://geotwn.vercel.app/) · [Watch the scripted agent](https://geotwn.vercel.app/?controller=mock) — select **After Hours** · [Inspect the live Jev trace](https://github.com/topherchris420/satellite-vision-scape/blob/main/docs/traces/jev-after-hours-live-2026-09-27.json) · [Source](https://github.com/topherchris420/satellite-vision-scape)

### R.A.I.N. Lab · Think with a room that can disagree

Bring a hypothesis or a difficult paper. Four constrained AI perspectives examine the evidence, feasibility, structure, and objections, leaving an inspectable record. Register an experiment, run it, and keep the outcome against the criteria written beforehand.

The local demo uses scripted dialogue and needs Python alone. Live meetings use a configured model.

[**Run the first meeting**](https://github.com/topherchris420/james_library#try-rain-lab) · [Browser interface](https://rainlabteam.vercel.app/) · [Experimental results](https://github.com/topherchris420/james_library/blob/main/RESULTS.md) · [Source](https://github.com/topherchris420/james_library)

### HumanTouch · Keep the texture of your voice

A writing studio for revising with AI while keeping your meaning, rhythm, and point of view. Steer the style, work from a voice profile, and shape variations from a source text. The writer keeps the final say.

## What the record says

The interesting part is what happens when the work meets a test.

- **A control can defeat a good decision.** In Pine Gap's paired scripted runs, oversized dial turns locked zero terminals in all ten runs; graded turns locked all four in all ten. That isolates a control-size effect in this simulation. [Experiment and raw traces](https://github.com/topherchris420/satellite-vision-scape/blob/main/experiments/results/tuning-control-magnitude/report.md).
- **A citation gate has a sharp edge.** R.A.I.N.'s recorded checks accepted verbatim quotes and rejected fabricated variants, but failed to accept genuine quotes with normalized typography. Both outcomes and their seeded reruns are published. [Results](https://github.com/topherchris420/james_library/blob/main/RESULTS.md).
- **An external comparison can say no.** DRR's preregistered structural-change test on NOAA's QBO series exceeded its false-alarm tolerance. That claim remains `NOT SUPPORTED`. [Protocol and result](https://github.com/topherchris420/dynamic-resonance-rooting/blob/main/docs/external-evidence.md).

Each result carries its scope. A simulation, a live agent session, and a physical measurement answer different questions.

## How I work

**Maximum conceptual ambition. Minimum credible experiment.**

Use AI aggressively to get to a working thing. Ask what a meaningful version could teach us within 72 hours. Write down the success condition, the failure condition, and what stays outside the claim. Run it, keep the record, and let the result shape the next build.

Models propose. Host code checks. People decide what deserves to happen next.
